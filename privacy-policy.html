GhostJob Buster Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 25, 2026
Developer: Coder-0X

1. Zero Data Collection Architecture

GhostJob Buster is built on a strict privacy-first foundation. The extension does not collect, record, transmit, or monetize your personal data. All candidate analysis and ghost job scoring takes place 100% locally in your browser memory.

2. Permissions and Scopes

Permission Technical Purpose
storage Saves candidate preferences and evaluated job history exclusively in local browser storage (chrome.storage.local).
alarms Schedules local periodic maintenance tasks (e.g. cache cleanup) within the background service worker.
https://*.linkedin.com/* Inspects publicly visible job descriptions to display candidate reality alerts directly on LinkedIn job cards.
https://*.indeed.com/* Inspects publicly visible job descriptions to display candidate reality alerts directly on Indeed job postings.

3. External Connections & Analytics

GhostJob Buster transmits zero telemetry or analytics data to external servers. It does not use Google Analytics, Sentry, or third-party trackers.

4. Source Code & Verification

GhostJob Buster is open-source. You can inspect the complete source code on GitHub at https://github.com/Coder-0X/ghostjob-buster.
